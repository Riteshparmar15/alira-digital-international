AI Agents & Automation FAQ

More answers on the FAQ page.

What is the difference between a basic chatbot and an AI agent?

A basic chatbot only replies to text questions. An AI agent can read incoming emails, PDFs or WhatsApp messages, search your internal catalog and SOPs, query your live ERP or SQL database via scoped APIs, draft quotations or purchase orders, and route edge cases to a human manager for approval.

Can an AI agent connect to our existing ERP, database or WhatsApp?

Yes. We build tool-calling integrations to custom ERPs, Laravel or Node backends, PostgreSQL/MySQL databases, WhatsApp Business API, Gmail/Outlook and accounting software—with strict read/write permissions per role.

How do you stop the AI from making mistakes or hallucinating prices?

Prices, stock numbers and customer discounts are never generated from AI memory. Our agents use strict JSON schema validation, query your live database for numbers, attach source citations to RAG answers, and trigger human-in-the-loop approval gates whenever confidence falls below threshold or quote value exceeds a set limit.

Is our company data used to train public AI models?

No. We deploy over enterprise zero-data-retention API agreements or private cloud instances so your quotations, pricing sheets, customer details and SOPs remain strictly yours and are never used to train external models.

How long does it take to build and test an AI agent system?

A focused workflow agent—such as automated RFQ extraction, WhatsApp lead triage or an internal SOP assistant—typically reaches shadow testing within 3 to 5 weeks, backed by an automated eval suite run against your real historical documents.

How do pricing and ongoing API costs work?

We provide a fixed engineering quotation after auditing your workflow and sample documents. Ongoing LLM/API costs are transparent and kept low (often cents per document) through smart model routing, prompt caching and token budgets.
