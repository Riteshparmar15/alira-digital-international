In short

Alira Digital designed and deployed a multi-agent AI operations copilot for Meridian Industrial Supply, a B2B distributor of process valves, flanges and industrial piping operating across Mumbai and Singapore. Incoming tender emails, WhatsApp enquiries and multi-page scanned PDF Bills of Quantities (BOQs) are automatically parsed, matched against 18,420 internal ERP SKUs using private hybrid RAG, checked against live dual-warehouse stock, and routed through deterministic pricing guardrails and human approval gates.

Before

Every tender BOQ was keyed in by hand across PDFs, WhatsApp and Excel.

Sales engineers received 140+ enquiries a day—some as 30-line scanned PDF tables, others as WhatsApp messages asking for Singapore vs. Mumbai stock. Each line had to be translated from customer wording into internal SKU codes, looked up across four Excel tabs, and typed into the ERP.

  • 89+ unread tender emails waiting in a shared sales inbox
  • 35 to 45 minutes per RFQ converting non-standard PDF descriptions
  • Manual WhatsApp pings to check Mumbai vs. Singapore warehouse stock
  • Copy-paste FX and margin mistakes on urgent multi-currency quotes

Client identities and reference numbers are blurred to protect customer confidentiality.

Architecture

What the multi-agent platform covers.

Built as a deterministic state graph with scoped ERP permissions, private retrieval and strict schema contracts at every handoff.

01

Omnichannel Intake Agent

Ingests Outlook, Gmail and WhatsApp RFQs, classifying urgency and customer tier automatically.

02

Vision-OCR BOQ Parser

Extracts multi-page valve, flange and piping tables into Pydantic-validated JSON rows.

03

Private Catalog RAG

Hybrid BM25 + pgvector search across 18,420 SKUs and engineering spec sheets with citations.

04

Dual-Hub ERP Stock Tool

Queries live Mumbai and Singapore inventory and computes air/sea split-dispatch plans.

05

Deterministic Pricing Engine

Applies customer tier rules, live INR/SGD/USD FX rates and 18% minimum margin floors.

06

Approval Gate Console

Holds high-value quotes or low-confidence items for 30-second human verification and dispatch.

07

WhatsApp Dispatch & Follow-Up

Sends branded PDF quotes with MTC 3.1 certificates and logs customer confirmations back to CRM.

08

Trace & Eval Observability

Tracks run latency, token cost per quote, human edits and 120 nightly safety benchmarks.

How accurate is the AI BOQ extraction and SKU matching?

Across 120 nightly benchmark RFQs and live production runs, the hybrid BM25 + pgvector RAG pipeline achieves 98.4% exact or semantic SKU match accuracy, automatically highlighting any uncertain substitution in amber for engineer confirmation.

Can the agent send incorrect pricing or discounts automatically?

No. Prices, tier discounts and the 18% minimum margin floor are enforced deterministically by querying the ERP database—never guessed by an LLM. Any quote over SGD 25,000 or with flagged items waits at the Approval Gate until a manager approves.

Does this replace the sales engineering team?

It eliminates repetitive PDF re-keying and multi-tab spreadsheet checks—cutting quote turnaround from 4.2 hours to 6.2 minutes—so engineers spend their time on technical specifications, negotiation and closing tenders.
